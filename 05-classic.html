Spaces to enjoy

Created with women in mind, Sultan Saba takes inspiration from nature’s balance and the beauty rituals of the East. Make time for yourself in Baku through hamam, facial and body care, beauty services and movement. The sound of water and a calm atmosphere invite a pause from the pace of the city.
